## Direct connections, with primary evidence

**Bohmann and Malkiewich** are coauthors with **Teena Gerhardt, Mona Merling, and Inna Zakharevich** of [A trace map on higher scissors congruence groups](https://arxiv.org/html/2303.08172v2). This is the clearest direct link between their two collections.

**Beaudry and Yeakel** are coauthors with **Maria Basterra, Kristine Bauer, Rosona Eldred, Brenda Johnson, and Mona Merling** of [Unbased calculus for functors to chain complexes](https://arxiv.org/abs/1409.1553).

**Beaudry, Chloe Lewis, Clover May, Sabrina Pauli, and Elizabeth Tatum** cite both versions of **Malkiewich's parametrized-spectra work** in [their equivariant parametrized cohomology guide](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.13971). This is a documented reference connection, not a claim of joint authorship of those Malkiewich papers.

The shared use of equivariance or operads does not establish equivalence between every theory in these notebooks. Genuine equivariant spectra, isovariant spaces, and RO(Pi B)-graded parametrized cohomology retain different data.

## Concrete exercises already explained in the notes

- Malkiewich: a common refinement of two rectangle dissections; ordinary cyclic-group homology as a separate algebraic calculation; a two-point-base family; rational versus irrational circle rotations as a periodic-point warm-up.
- Bohmann: A(C2)=Z[t]/(t²-2t); restriction versus additive transfer; the finite-set norm N(k)=k+binomial(k,2)t; graded basis sizes for LS3 without an unsupported claim about the loop product.
- Yeakel: a reflection-equivariant collapse that fails isovariance; a cubic self-map separating group stabilizers from self-map fixed points; an elementary quadratic cross-effect; finite injection counts and why the indexing category is not merely N.
- Beaudry: a dual-number-algebra Ext tower; Adams-chart bidegree bookkeeping; sign local coefficients on S1; arithmetic of the stated exotic Picard subgroup; low-dimensional consequences of the matrix-product-state classifying-space type.

These are written exercises and design observations, not a claim that new executable software, formal proofs, or visualizations were added.
