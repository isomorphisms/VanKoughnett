### Names and roles

A name printed only with initials is retained in that form unless an inspected source supplies an unambiguous expansion. Different spellings in historical records are not evidence of different people, and matching surnames are not evidence of the same person. We do not infer gender or publish private contact information. Publicly established name corrections should be incorporated when verified.

Appendix authors, translators, editors, foreword writers, and explicitly named contributors are included below when the bibliography names them. Their role is indicated rather than silently promoting them to authors of the whole book. Entries marked 'in preparation' are preserved as historical credits, not asserted to have appeared in print.

Only bibliographic facts and names are reproduced here, not copyrighted abstracts, proofs, or full texts.

## Remaining coverage, stated plainly

The research inventories contain more works than the seven bibliographies above. No complete traversal has yet been made of all Malkiewich papers and the textbook draft, all Bohmann papers, every Yeakel research paper and dissertation, or the full Beaudry chromatic and mathematical-physics corpus. Their direct authors and located collaborators are thanked in the notebooks, but individual bibliography-level coverage for those remaining works is not claimed.

A later extension should add one source-specific table per inspected bibliography, with the exact version and every printed person retained. It should resolve identities using public scholarly evidence, preserve corrections and author-order changes, and avoid merging merely similar names. Credit does not require falsely claiming that the cited work itself has been read.
