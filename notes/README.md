## Reading conventions

- Spaces are pointed CW complexes or another well-behaved model of pointed spaces.
- \(\Sigma X=S^1\wedge X\) and \(\Omega X=\operatorname{Map}_*(S^1,X)\).
- \([X,Y]_*\) denotes pointed homotopy classes of maps.
- \(\mathbb S\) is the sphere spectrum; \(\mathrm{Sp}\) denotes the stable homotopy theory/category of spectra.
- Formulas about \(\pi_k^s\) and suspension have hypotheses; a stable equivalence need not be a levelwise equivalence.
- “Course outline” and “lecture notes” are distinguished from direct video observations.
