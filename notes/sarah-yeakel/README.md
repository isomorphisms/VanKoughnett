## Important correction

Yeakel explicitly warns that her thesis and older user's guide contain errors involving cross-effects. Her revised paper and eventual publication concern a **lax monoidal model for multilinearization**; the old guide's stronger statement about Goodwillie derivatives must not be treated as the corrected theorem. See the [author's warning](https://sites.google.com/view/syeakel/research), [arXiv version history](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.06915), and the research note's section 3.

The dissertation's institutional title is *Goodwillie calculus and I*; the author's link calls it *Goodwillie calculus and injections*. The I denotes finite sets and injections. It is not evidence for an identification of the remembered “K-bar spectrum.”

## Relation to this repository

The existing seminar notes establish suspension, fibers/cofibers, and spectra. Yeakel supplies a route toward functor calculus and operations; the wider guide collection branches into equivariant and chromatic subjects. The new material is independent study writing, not a claim that these topics were covered in a particular VanKoughnett recording.

Thank you to Sarah Yeakel and all the named authors for making their work and explanations available. Original PDFs and scans stay with their hosts; this repository contains summaries, examples, source links, and explicit reading limits.
